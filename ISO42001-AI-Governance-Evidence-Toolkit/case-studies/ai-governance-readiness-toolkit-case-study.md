# AI Governance Readiness Toolkit - Case Study

## Problem

Small and medium-sized organisations often adopt AI tools before governance evidence is ready.

## Solution

The toolkit provides a practical evidence baseline with:

- AI use-case inventory,
- AI risk register,
- human oversight log,
- supplier review checklist,
- AI policy and impact assessment examples,
- governance response template,
- and a generated readiness report.

## Outcome

The toolkit supports ISO42001-style readiness conversations and helps translate AI governance concepts into evidence-ready artefacts.

## Portfolio value

Relevant for AI Governance, Cyber GRC, EU AI Act readiness and responsible AI implementation roles.
