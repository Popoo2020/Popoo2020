# Consulting Offer: AI Governance Readiness Assessment for SMEs

## Service

**AI Governance Readiness Assessment**

A practical, lightweight assessment for small and medium-sized organisations that already use AI tools, or plan to adopt AI, but do not yet have a clear governance structure, use-case inventory, risk assessment or evidence trail.

## Best for

- SMEs using ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Shopify AI, AI customer support or AI marketing tools,
- startups preparing for enterprise customers or investor due diligence,
- e-commerce teams using AI for product content and support workflows,
- organisations that want a practical first step toward ISO/IEC 42001-style AI management-system thinking,
- teams that need AI policy, risk ownership and human review expectations.

## Typical client problem

The organisation is already using AI, but leadership cannot clearly answer:

- Which AI tools are in use?
- Who owns each AI use case?
- What data is entered into AI tools?
- Which outputs require human review?
- Which suppliers/tools are approved?
- What risks have been documented?
- What evidence exists for audit, customer or management review?

## Deliverables

A typical engagement can produce:

1. AI use-case register,
2. AI risk register,
3. AI impact assessment for one selected workflow,
4. draft internal AI-use policy,
5. AI supplier due-diligence checklist,
6. human oversight decision log template,
7. readiness report with gaps, owners and recommended next steps.

## Suggested format

| Phase | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake call and AI use-case discovery | Initial AI use-case list |
| 2 | Documentation and workflow review | Risk and evidence notes |
| 3 | Governance artefact drafting | Policy, register and assessment templates |
| 4 | Readiness report | Prioritised roadmap and next steps |
| 5 | Optional workshop | Staff/management awareness session |

## Timeline

A lightweight version can be delivered as a short remote engagement over one to two weeks, depending on client availability and scope.

## Example scope

**Starter package**

- up to 5 AI use cases,
- one impact assessment example,
- one supplier/tool review,
- draft policy,
- final readiness summary.

**Expanded package**

- more use cases,
- multiple workflows,
- supplier review expansion,
- staff awareness workshop,
- governance roadmap and recurring review process.

## Out of scope

This service does not provide:

- legal advice,
- formal ISO/IEC 42001 certification,
- external audit opinion,
- production security testing,
- procurement approval on behalf of the client,
- final regulatory classification without qualified legal/compliance review.

I help SMEs adopt AI safely, responsibly and commercially by turning AI governance into practical documentation, risk visibility, human oversight and evidence-ready workflows.
