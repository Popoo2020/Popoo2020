# EU AI Act Readiness: Risk Classification Note

> Practical readiness note for SME AI governance discussions. This is not legal advice and does not replace legal or regulatory assessment.

## Purpose

This note helps organisations start a structured conversation about how AI use cases may need to be reviewed under the EU AI Act risk-based approach. It is designed to support early readiness, not to provide final legal classification.

## Working classification model

| Category | Practical meaning | Example SME indicators | Suggested action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimal or low risk | Low-impact AI assistance with human review | Brainstorming, summarising public material, drafting marketing text | Manage through AI policy and human review |
| Limited transparency risk | Users may need to know they are interacting with AI or AI-generated content | Chatbot, AI-generated customer content, synthetic media | Add transparency, review and disclosure controls |
| High-risk indicators | AI may affect access, opportunities, safety, rights or regulated decisions | Employment support, education access, financial eligibility, safety-related systems | Escalate to legal/compliance review before use |
| Prohibited-risk red flags | Use case may involve unacceptable manipulation, exploitation or harmful social scoring patterns | Coercive profiling, exploitative manipulation, unacceptable biometric or scoring patterns | Do not deploy without specialist legal review |

## Readiness questions

For each AI use case, ask:

1. What decision or workflow does the AI support?
2. Who is affected by the output?
3. Is the AI output used only as a draft, or does it influence a real decision?
4. Does the use case involve personal data, vulnerable groups or sensitive contexts?
5. Is the system used in employment, education, access control, credit, safety, public services or similar high-impact areas?
6. Is a human decision-maker clearly responsible?
7. Can the organisation explain the purpose, limitations and review process?
8. Is there evidence of risk assessment, human oversight and monitoring?

## Example mapping

| Use case | Initial readiness classification | Reason | Recommended handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI-assisted Shopify product descriptions | Low to limited transparency risk | Customer-facing content may be inaccurate or misleading if unchecked | Human review and marketing-claim verification |
| Customer-support response drafting | Limited transparency / medium operational risk | AI draft may affect customer communication | Staff review before sending and escalation path |
| Internal policy summarisation | Low risk | Internal drafting support with source verification | Link to source and manager review |
| Recruitment note assistance | High-risk indicator | Employment-related context and potential fairness impact | Do not deploy without formal legal/compliance review |
| Security workflow summarisation | Medium to high operational risk | AI may influence security workflow handling | Human approval gate and audit trail |

## Evidence to maintain

Recommended evidence for readiness:

- AI use-case register,
- business purpose and owner,
- risk assessment,
- human oversight requirement,
- data minimisation approach,
- supplier/tool review,
- transparency/disclosure note where relevant,
- periodic review record,
- incident or complaint handling procedure.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate to legal, compliance or a qualified specialist if the AI use case:

- affects employment, education, credit, access to services or safety,
- profiles or ranks people,
- uses sensitive or vulnerable-group data,
- automates or strongly influences decisions about individuals,
- operates without meaningful human review,
- produces content that users may mistake for human-generated or verified information,
- connects directly to operational systems or write actions.

## Consultant note

For client-facing work, this note should be used as an intake and triage artefact. Final classification should be confirmed by the client's legal/compliance function or external counsel where material risk exists.
