# AI Incident and Complaint Handling Template

> Practical template for AI governance readiness. This is not legal advice and should be adapted to sector, contractual and regulatory requirements.

## Purpose

This template helps organisations document, triage and respond to AI-related incidents, complaints or quality concerns. It supports traceability, accountability and continuous improvement.

## Incident type

Select all that apply:

- inaccurate or misleading AI output,
- harmful, biased or unfair output,
- confidentiality or data-minimisation concern,
- unauthorised AI tool use,
- missing human review,
- customer or employee complaint,
- supplier/tool behaviour change,
- operational workflow error,
- other.

## Description

Describe what happened in plain language.

```text
What happened?
Who or what was affected?
How was it discovered?
What evidence is available?
```

## Root-cause review

Potential contributing factors:

- unclear policy,
- insufficient human review,
- poor prompt or workflow design,
- unapproved tool use,
- supplier/tool change,
- incomplete data minimisation,
- inadequate user training,
- missing ownership or escalation path.

## Closure checklist

Before closing the incident, confirm:

- the immediate risk has been contained,
- relevant stakeholders were informed,
- corrective actions were assigned,
- policy/register/control documents were updated if needed,
- lessons learned were documented,
- evidence is stored in the governance record.

## Lessons learned

```text
What should change in policy, training, supplier review, human oversight or technical controls?
```
