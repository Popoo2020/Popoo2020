# AI Governance Readiness Toolkit - Executive Case Study

## Problem

Many small and medium-sized organisations want to adopt AI tools responsibly and prepare for ISO/IEC 42001-style governance, but they often lack practical governance documentation.

Common gaps include:

- no clear AI system inventory,
- no documented AI risk register,
- unclear human oversight expectations,
- limited supplier due diligence,
- no AI incident or complaint handling template,
- and no evidence-ready readiness report for management review.

## Solution

Built an evidence-based AI Governance Readiness Toolkit containing:

- AI use-case inventory,
- AI risk register,
- human oversight decision log,
- supplier due-diligence checklist,
- AI policy draft,
- AI impact assessment example,
- AI incident and complaint handling template,
- EU AI Act readiness note,
- client-facing consulting offer,
- simulated client-style case study,
- and a readiness report generator.

## Outcome

The toolkit generates a structured readiness report that supports governance gap analysis, management discussion and ISO/IEC 42001-oriented preparation.

The project demonstrates how AI governance can be translated from abstract regulation into practical, evidence-ready workflows for SMEs.

## Employer value

This case study demonstrates practical capability in:

- AI governance documentation,
- cybersecurity GRC thinking,
- ISO/IEC 42001 readiness,
- EU AI Act readiness conversations,
- responsible AI implementation,
- supplier and tool review,
- and portfolio-grade consulting communication.

## Important limitation

This is a portfolio-grade readiness example. It is not legal advice, certification evidence or a formal ISO/IEC 42001 audit.
