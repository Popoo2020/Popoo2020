# Hiring Manager Summary

## What this project demonstrates

ISO42001-AI-Governance-Evidence-Toolkit demonstrates practical AI governance readiness work for SMEs adopting AI tools.

The project translates AI governance concepts into concrete artefacts: use-case inventory, risk register, AI policy, supplier due diligence, human oversight, incident handling, EU AI Act readiness notes, client-facing offer, case study and generated readiness report.

## Relevant roles

- AI Governance Consultant
- Cybersecurity GRC Consultant
- Responsible AI Specialist
- ISO/IEC 42001 Readiness Advisor
- EU AI Act Readiness Support
- AI Implementation Consultant for SMEs

## What this demonstrates

- Ability to turn governance ideas into practical documentation.
- Awareness of human oversight and supplier/tool review.
- Practical connection between ISO 27001, ISO/IEC 42001 and AI adoption.
- Client-facing consulting packaging.
- Python-based readiness report generation.
- CI validation and unit tests for the report generator.

## Business value

The project shows how a company can move from informal AI adoption to documented, owned, risk-assessed and evidence-backed AI use.

## Current limitations

This is a portfolio and readiness toolkit, not legal advice, a formal EU AI Act classification tool, a complete ISO/IEC 42001 implementation package or certification evidence.
