# Changelog

All notable changes to the ISO42001 AI Governance Evidence Toolkit are documented in this file.

## [0.1.0] - Portfolio Readiness Release

### Added
- AI use-case register sample.
- AI risk register sample.
- Governance control mapping sample.
- Human oversight decision log.
- AI supplier review checklist.
- AI policy draft.
- AI impact assessment example.
- AI incident and complaint handling template.
- EU AI Act readiness note.
- Sundai AI-style client case study.
- Readiness report generator.
- Unit tests and CI validation.
- CodeQL and Dependabot configuration.
- Architecture documentation.
- Threat model.
- Demo output documentation.
- Quality and security checklist.
- Makefile validation target.
- Version notes.

### Security
- Sample-data-only posture documented.
- Clear limitations around legal advice, certification and formal audit status.
- Quality and security checklist added for employer/client review.
- Human oversight and supplier-review considerations documented.

### Known limitations
- Portfolio readiness toolkit, not a complete management system.
- No persistent evidence store.
- No role-based access control.
- Markdown-only report output.
- Real organisations should adapt the templates to their context.
