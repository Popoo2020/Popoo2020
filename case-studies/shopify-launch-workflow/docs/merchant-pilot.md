# One product, one supplier, one market

### The buyer

A merchant already has a product and a potential supplier. They are considering a new sales country, or still copying orders and tracking numbers between tools. A Shopify agency with a merchant in this position can sponsor the same pilot.

### What SundAI can offer

**Can this product support paid acquisition and a reliable delivery promise in your next market?** Bring one SKU, a supplier quote, a test destination and the budget you are willing to put at risk. We calculate the acquisition-cost ceiling, stage a draft catalog item and rehearse a direct-to-customer order including a duplicate and a shipment exception. You receive a written launch or hold decision, the calculation and a handover your operator can repeat.

The merchant provides its legal entity and target market, price and cost assumptions, supplier mapping, documented delivery conditions, approved product facts and a named reviewer. Agree a fixed scope and fee after checking API access. The deliverables are the margin and cash model, a draft product, one supplier integration rehearsal, a campaign test brief and the data/security handover. Spending, legal advice, tax determination, live fulfilment, advertising management and extra markets are separately scoped.

### Where the opportunity is

Shopify already generates store designs from a prompt. Atlas offers AI-built pages and merchandising, DSers handles supplier order automation and tracking, and a Shopify App Store listing offers landed-cost and margin calculations. Dropday even offers integrations to private supplier APIs. Parameters, AI copy, MCP or a margin calculator on their own do not establish exclusivity.

What a buyer can compare is the price of a wrong launch decision and the evidence available before spending: which entity and shipping lane were reviewed, how much can be spent to acquire a customer, whether the quoted SKU actually routes, what happens on retries, and which customer fields the model sees. The owner gets the implementation and a decision to proceed or stop. A public merchant question about supplier APIs and a separate Make fulfilment request support the existence of this operational problem. They do not measure willingness to pay SundAI.
