# From a new Shopify store to the first delivery

A merchant can use this walkthrough to prepare one product for one destination. The repository reproduces the commercial decision, draft product, campaign brief and supplier order rehearsal. The merchant completes the Shopify admin, payment, supplier and storefront steps in their own accounts. The public example uses fictional inputs throughout.

| Step | Who does the work | What to verify before going on |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Choose the product and market | The owner brings the selling price, cash ceiling, available product sample and a dated supplier quote for a named SKU and destination. Record where the entity is registered and where the parcel actually leaves from. Assign someone to handle returns and set a delivery promise. | The supplier confirms the variant, unit cost, shipping cost, dispatch origin and quoted time. Record the expiry date and currency. A missing quote or untested sample goes back to sourcing. |
| 2. Establish the store | The merchant owns the Shopify account and domain, enables multi-factor authentication, and grants the implementer access appropriate to the task. Set the store details, customer contact address, currency, language and target market. Configure the payment provider using the entity's eligibility and the merchant's payout details. | The owner can recover the store and domain, the right currency appears, and the payment provider has confirmed the relevant account status. Keep verification documents in the provider's portal. Record only the review outcome in the project brief. |
| 3. Decide whether to spend | Enter cash, setup, sample cost, supplier float, payment fees, tax/return allowances and planned acquisition cost in a copy of the [brief](../examples/brief.json). Run the rehearsal with the matched supplier fixtures or run the `build` command for a planning-only result. | The [sample report](../examples/results/ready.md) shows the cost ceiling for acquiring one customer and cash required before sales. If the decision is `HOLD`, use the corrective action before staging a product or allocating campaign spend. |
| 4. Set up the catalog and delivery route | Map the Shopify SKU to the supplier's fulfilment variant and artwork. Use the draft CSV or optional development-store command. Configure the market, sales channel, shipping profile, locations and zones. Review the theme on mobile, the product facts, an example address, shipping charge, delivery promise, returns and contact page. | The target customer sees the agreed currency, product, delivery charge and support details. Try an address outside the supported lane and verify it cannot obtain a misleading shipping offer. Get the merchant's approval of the factual product copy. |
| 5. Review people, data and marketing | Follow the [security and legal decision record](security-privacy-ai.md). Record who receives each field, how long it is kept and how an order can be found or deleted when appropriate. Make a no-spend campaign draft and set the owner's acquisition-cost ceiling. | Test the regional privacy controls in separate test sessions, including manually added pixels. Keep synthetic checkout data in tests and approved product facts in the AI client. Record a reviewer, test date and outcome privately. |
| 6. Test checkout and a duplicate delivery | Use an isolated test store, a payment gateway in test mode, and a supplier sandbox or disconnected fulfilment app. Exercise a successful payment, a decline, cancellation and receipt. Run the local `rehearse` command to repeat a signed paid-order event. | The decline creates no supplier commitment. There is one draft after repeated delivery of the same event. Check receipt currency and totals, the visible shipping rate, tracking and a failed fulfilment. Capture a sanitized discrepancy report for anything unresolved. |
| 7. Approve release and reconcile the first order | The merchant authorizes live payments, product publication, supplier ordering and any advertising separately. Assign an operator to review the first paid order. A live connector must persist the supplier reference and reconcile an uncertain response before retrying. | Match Shopify payment, supplier acceptance, actual invoice, customer tracking and delivery outcome. If a discrepancy appears, pause the affected route or campaign, keep the evidence and have the assigned operator reconcile before resuming. |

### Handover and measurement

Keep a private sign-off record with the owner, date, store environment, SKU and redacted evidence for each step. Give the operator the product-to-supplier mapping, margin assumptions, merchant report, procedure for duplicates or missed tracking, and supplier support path. A technical rehearsal can pass while the merchant still needs to resolve a payout, returns or product-specific question.

After launch, compare actual supplier invoices, payment fees, returns and acquisition spend with the assumptions in the [report](../examples/results/ready.md). Count fulfilled orders, repeat events that caused a second supplier order, and staff minutes per exception. For marketing, record spend and attributed new customers over the same period. With zero acquired customers, the cost per acquisition is undefined. Keep fixed overhead separate when presenting per-order contribution. Record any measured results only with the merchant's permission.
