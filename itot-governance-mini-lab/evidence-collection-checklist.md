# Evidence Collection Checklist

This checklist helps translate security and compliance requirements into practical evidence.

## Asset and ownership evidence

- Asset inventory exists.
- Asset owner is defined.
- Business function is documented.
- Criticality is documented.
- Network zone is documented.

## Access evidence

- Access owner is identified.
- Privileged access is documented.
- Remote access rules are documented.
- Exceptions have an owner and review date.
- Access review records are retained.

## Change evidence

- Change reason is documented.
- Implementation owner is documented.
- Approval or review is recorded.
- Rollback or recovery consideration is documented where relevant.

## Logging evidence

- Logging expectations are defined.
- Responsible reviewer is assigned.
- Critical event types are identified.
- Retention expectations are documented.

## Incident response evidence

- Escalation path is documented.
- Contact points are identified.
- Evidence handling expectations are described.
- Lessons learned are recorded after incidents.
