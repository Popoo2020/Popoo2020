# IT/OT Security Policy Outline

This outline shows how an IT/OT security policy can be structured for regulated technical environments.

## 1. Scope

Define which business units, sites, systems, networks and operational environments are covered.

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

- Business owner
- IT owner
- OT owner
- Security owner
- System owner
- External supplier or service provider

## 3. Asset governance

- Maintain an asset inventory.
- Assign an owner to each critical asset.
- Record business function and criticality.
- Review inventory accuracy periodically.

## 4. Access management

- Use named accounts where possible.
- Limit privileged access.
- Document approvals.
- Review access regularly.
- Define emergency access procedures.

## 5. Change management

- Document business reason and system owner.
- Assess operational impact.
- Plan rollback where relevant.
- Record implementation and approval.

## 6. Logging and monitoring

- Define what should be logged.
- Assign log review responsibility.
- Protect logs from unauthorised modification.
- Review critical events.

## 7. Incident response

- Define escalation paths.
- Identify system owners and contact points.
- Document evidence handling.
- Review lessons learned after incidents.

## 8. Supplier and remote access

- Define supplier access rules.
- Use approval and time limitation.
- Log remote access sessions where possible.
- Review external access periodically.

## 9. Review cycle

The policy should be reviewed at least annually or after major changes to systems, ownership, architecture or regulatory requirements.
